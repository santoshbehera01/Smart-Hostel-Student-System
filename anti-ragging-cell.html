Anti Ragging Cell

Einstein Academy of Technology and Management — A Ragging-Free Campus

Ragging in any form is strictly prohibited inside the hostel premises and across the campus, in accordance with the UGC Regulations on Curbing the Menace of Ragging in Higher Educational Institutions, 2009. Any student found involved in ragging — physical, verbal, or online — will face strict disciplinary action, which may include suspension or expulsion.

What Counts as Ragging

  • Any act that causes physical, psychological, or mental harm to a fresher or junior student.
  • Forcing a student to do something they are unwilling to do, or that causes embarrassment or fear.
  • Teasing, abusing, or playing practical jokes that cause mental or physical trouble.

How to Report

Any incident of ragging can be reported immediately to the Hostel Superintendent, any member of the Hostel Committee, or through the numbers listed on the Emergency Contact page. All complaints are treated in strict confidence.
