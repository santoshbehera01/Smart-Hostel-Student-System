Rules and Regulations for the Hostel

Einstein Academy of Technology and Management, Baniatangi, Bajpur, Khordha

  1. All the boarders must attend the classes regularly. Minimum 75% attendance in each subject is mandatory to appear the university examination.
  2. Hostel will be locked during class hours. No one is permitted to stay in the hostel during class hours, failing which disciplinary action as deemed fit will be initiated against the defaulters.
  3. In case of medical emergency, the sick room may be availed by the boarders with prior approval.
  4. All the hostel boarders must be in proper uniform with shoes, tie, and ID cards while attending the classes.
  5. All the boarders are advised to keep their rooms and hostel premises neat and clean.
  6. Temporary absence application must be submitted to the care-taker before leaving the hostel. The application must be signed by the concerned HOD and the Hostel Superintendent.
  7. Study hours must be properly maintained inside the hostel. The study hour timing is from 6:00 PM to 9:00 PM in the evening.
  8. Day scholars are not permitted to enter and stay in the hostel.
  9. Use of loud speakers inside the hostel premises is strictly prohibited.
  10. Ragging is strictly prohibited. If any boarders are found to be involved in ragging, disciplinary action as deemed fit will be initiated against them.
  11. Any type of indisciplinary activities (smoking / drinking / chewing tobacco / misbehaving / spitting inside the hostel premises) will be considered a serious offence, and a penalty of Rs. 10,000 will be imposed against the defaulters.
  12. Visitors (parents/guardians) can meet their wards only during visiting hours (6 AM to 8 AM in the morning and 4 PM to 6 PM in the evening) by entering their names and purpose of visit in the visiting register.
  13. Nobody is allowed to go to the top of the roof under any circumstances.
  14. All hostel boarders are advised to take good care of their belongings; the college will not be responsible for any mishaps (theft/loss).
  15. All boarders are advised to stay in their rooms during the time when night attendance is taken.

— Director
